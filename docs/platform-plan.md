# Platform Plan: Astrology & Simulation Theory Teaching

## Overview

A plan for building a sustainable teaching platform around astrology and simulation theory, aimed at making the work accessible to people who are looking for it — without conversion, marketing pressure, or pursuit of external validation. Money exists to sustain the work, not as the goal.

## Core Positioning & Guiding Principles

The stance is "make it available for those who are ready" — not "build the biggest audience possible." This is a meaningful constraint that shapes every other decision and is well-suited to mediums where seekers can find work, and poorly suited to push-based marketing.

**Principles that follow from the stance:**

- Findability over marketing. Make sure people searching for these intersections can locate the work; don't push the work at people who aren't looking.
- No urgency tactics, funnel marketing, or "limited time" pricing. These contradict the stated values and would erode trust with the audience the work is meant to reach.
- Pricing should be honest — enough that the work is sustainable, not so high it gates access for sincere seekers. A pay-what-you-can option for those without means aligns with the values and is common among teachers in adjacent traditions.
- The audience builds slowly. That's the correct outcome. The right people finding the work over years matters more than fast growth.

## The Voice-First Workflow (Solve This First)

Writing aversion is a real constraint. The workflow turns talking into the source material for everything else:

1. Record audio (podcast episode, voice memo, monologue).
2. Run it through Descript or Riverside for transcription and transcript-based editing.
3. Use AI tools to draft the small amount of written material the site needs (show notes, episode descriptions, occasional blog posts) from the transcript — review and approve rather than write from scratch.

One hour of talking becomes a podcast episode, YouTube upload, show notes, and any written material needed. Without this workflow, the writing requirement will kill the project. With it, talking drives the entire content engine.

## Content Strategy

**Podcast as the home base.** Long-form, unhurried, willing to sit with ideas. Hosted on standard platforms (Spotify, Apple Podcasts) so it appears in searches. Weekly or biweekly cadence.

**YouTube as the discovery layer.** Same audio with simple visuals. This matters because YouTube is where people actually search for things like "simulation theory and consciousness" or "astrology and the nature of reality." Long-form (30-60 min) videos compound in search for years. Searchable titles matter — they're how the right people find the work.

**Simple website.** One to three pages: who he is, the framework, how to work with him (consultations, eventually membership), and a way to contact. Doesn't need to be elaborate. A recorded video intro can substitute for written "about me" copy.

**What he should not do:** aggressive short-form social media, paid advertising, growth hacking, or anything designed to push the message at people who haven't sought it out. These would work, but they contradict the stance and would attract the wrong audience.

## Monetization Model

All offerings should be entirely opt-in by people who've self-selected as ready.

**Consultations (the income engine, especially early).** One-on-one sessions for people who want to go deeper with their own charts and questions. Priced honestly — meaningful enough that the work sustains him, accessible enough for sincere seekers. Consider a sliding scale or limited pay-what-you-can slots.

**Membership / subscription (medium-term).** Once a podcast audience exists, a paid tier (Substack, Patreon, or private feed) for monthly deeper teachings, Q&As, or whatever form makes sense. Even a small membership produces meaningful recurring revenue. This compounds.

**Cohort-based courses (longer-term).** Live, time-bounded group programs on specific aspects of the framework. Higher price point justified by the depth and live access. The people who show up are by definition ready.

**Eventual podcast revenue.** Sponsorships only become meaningful at ~5-10k downloads per episode. Not a near-term factor. Listener support (e.g., a tip jar or membership) is more aligned with the values and can start earlier.

## Differentiation

The intersection of astrology and simulation theory is genuinely distinctive. Most astrologers don't engage simulation theory explicitly; most simulation theory voices come from tech or philosophy and don't touch astrology. Sitting at that intersection is real positioning — provided the framework that connects them is articulated clearly.

Before launching, worth spending time recording (just for himself) what he actually believes about how astrology and simulation theory relate. Out of that, the core teachings of the show emerge, and the language of the website follows.

## First 90 Days

**Weeks 1-4: Internal foundation.**
- Record 5-10 hours of monologue articulating the framework, just for clarification. Not for release.
- Out of that, identify the core teachings and the shape of the show.
- Buy the gear: Shure MV7 or SM7B, basic audio interface, decent headphones. ~$300-700 total.

**Weeks 5-8: Build the catalog before launch.**
- Record 6-8 podcast episodes. Don't launch with one episode — launch with a small library so people who arrive have something to go through.
- Build the simple website. One page can work.
- Set up booking and payment (Acuity or Calendly + Stripe) for consultations.

**Weeks 9-12: Launch quietly.**
- Publish the podcast on standard platforms (Spotify, Apple, YouTube).
- Open consultation bookings at honest prices.
- Don't push it. Let it exist. People searching for these intersections will start finding it.
- Pay attention to what listeners ask about — that shapes future episodes.

After 90 days: continue the rhythm. The membership and courses follow later, once audience demand makes sense. If the audience stays small, the consultations and podcast can sustain themselves at a smaller scale — also a fine outcome.

## Sustainability Notes

**This is a long-horizon path.** Indie teachers in this space who last typically take 3-5 years to reach financial stability. Plan financially for that. The consultation income can carry things during the audience-building phase.

**Avoid burnout patterns.** Underpricing and giving endless free time are the most common ways mission-driven teachers wash out within two years. The healthy version of "money serves the message" looks like charging enough to do this full-time without resentment, having clear capacity limits, and reinvesting revenue into making the work better.

**The work compounds slowly.** Most growth comes from listeners sharing episodes with one specific friend who'd resonate, not from going viral. This is the right pattern for the audience he's trying to reach.

## Optional: Sharpening the Work

Independent of audience-building, the quality of the teachings improves dramatically when the framework has been pressure-tested against the strongest versions of adjacent thinking — serious philosophers of mind, contemplative traditions with developed metaphysics, other thinkers who've worked at this intersection. Not for validation, but because listeners who'll find value in the work will mostly be people who've read widely themselves, and they'll connect more deeply with teachings that show evidence of having engaged the broader conversation. Worth considering as an ongoing practice, not a prerequisite to launching.

## Open Decisions

A few things to clarify before or during early execution:

- The specific framework: how astrology and simulation theory relate in his model. Sharpening this changes everything downstream.
- Visual identity: the spiritual/metaphysical space defaults to purple gradients and starfields. A distinctive aesthetic stands out a lot and signals "serious teacher."
- Hours per week he wants to spend on consultations vs. content creation. Affects pricing and capacity.
- Whether to be on video (YouTube long-form) or audio-only. Both work; video opens more discovery paths.
