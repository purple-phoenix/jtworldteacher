And so the myth is that there are some people whose knowledge is extensive enough that it made sense to try to keep their brains alive and their heads alive so that you can communicate with them in some way and get access to that knowledge. But the urban myth goes on to say that they all went nuts, and what really made them crazy was that they had phantom experiences. Like a person who is an amputee has phantom limb experiences, but that the heads that they were keeping alive or trying to keep alive were having all kinds of phantom experiences that were driving them nuts, particularly phantom thirst. That even though they were in a solution and very well hydrated, that they were experiencing a phantom thirst that eventually drove them crazy.
