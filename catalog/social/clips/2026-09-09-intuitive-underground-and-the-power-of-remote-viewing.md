### 3. Never trust a psychic with zero unhappy clients

- **Time:** `01:47:58.020` → `01:49:15.700` (78 s)
- **Speaker:** Morgan Farrell
- **Why it works:** A practical, contrarian red-flag warning that anyone who has thought about hiring a psychic can use, and it ends on a punchline ("no better than a cold read").
- **Caption:** A professional remote viewer's warning: if a psychic says no client has ever been unhappy with their work, they're probably just telling people what they want to hear.
- **Quote card:** "Be wary of any team who tells you that they've never had a client displeased with the results."
- **Hashtags:** #RemoteViewing #Psychic #RedFlags #Intuition

> Be cautious of any, whether they're just your everyday psychic medium or a professional team of remote viewers or self-proclaimed team of professionals: be wary of any team who tells you that they've never had a client displeased with the results, because that's an instant red flag to me right there… This is a known phenomenon in psychic work and remote viewing. If you haven't trained and gained enough experience, what can happen is the person can come up with psychic information that is basically what the client wants to hear… It's something that an operator can train themselves not to do, but very few actually do that. And hence the warning: if a psychic or remote viewer tells you that they've never had a client displeased with their work, then it's very likely that's what they're doing. They're just giving them the fluffy results that they want to hear, which at the end of the day, that's no better than a cold read.
