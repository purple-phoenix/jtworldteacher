## About this episode

The "crusade" thesis is that UFOX and ufology spaces are being reshaped by a sudden influx of overtly Catholic voices, a synchronicity that JT treats as a possible counter-move by the Vatican against the older Protestant-evangelical / intelligence-community grip on the UFO narrative. The trigger event was a group of evangelical pastors emerging to claim they'd been briefed by intel insiders that disclosure would reveal "interdimensional demonic UFOs masquerading as aliens" — a story Rep. Burleson and Perry Stone walked back. Within hours, Pastor Alan DiDio was "cocooned" in spaces with Red Panda Koala and Tupacabra as co-hosts, narrative locked in. JT reads this as standard SIOP (military marketing) tradecraft.

The "new management" angle is Mav (Catholic, 9/11 first responder) and Gene Stickle (Sunday Syndicate) running spaces that eclipse UFOX itself — coast-to-coast-AM-style, intellectually serious, Catholic-framed, and free of UFOX's recurring drama. The "tailspin" is UFOX itself: mostly drama, the Tupa/Red gatekeeping cocoon, the "UFO hate group" rehash, weaponized information, and a habit of debunking or co-opting any newcomer's statements within hours.
