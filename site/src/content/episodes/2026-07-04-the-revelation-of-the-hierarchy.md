## About this episode

This is the ground-floor episode — the "broad overview we never really did before." JT reworks his recurring Spaces into a structured, interview-driven podcast called **Peak Mind**, with his brother Matt (a self-described math guy) asking the questions and keeping it moving. The name isn't about JT; it's about the period we've just lived through: roughly late 2012 to 2019, the era of peak aggregate human attention, funneled through social media to the same handful of archetypes and events each day. That sustained global focus, JT argues, cohered reality into a legible set of patterns — and it's the backdrop against which a real awakening is now becoming possible for some (not all) people, as we emerge out the other side.

From there he unspools the whole cosmology. **The revelation of the hierarchy** is a prediction inherited from Theosophy — the last major clairvoyant community, launched when Western Europeans reached Tibet in the 1860s — which peaked in the 1920s–30s with a forecast for right now: an interstellar object entering the inner solar system around 2025, carried as the Hindu Kalki avatar on a "higher dimension," transmitting a signal that tips civilization into a reset (JT ties it to the red-clad rider on the white horse in Revelation). The revelation itself is twofold: the return of the "gods" (little-g — what JT calls the **archons**) into human consciousness, and the exposure of an internal, unconscious network of governance among humans that sits beneath presidents, kings, and votes.

The metaphysical spine is simulation on **biological hardware** — not a computer game but a Matrix-style overlay generated on a network of living brains, which is what Jung was actually pointing at with the collective unconscious. The **archons** are the Gnostic hijackers who dropped humanity into the simulation and installed our vices, which we then project outward onto the planets (Mars "causing" aggression is really Mars used as a clock for a transpersonal pattern we can't see directly). The **Pitris** — Hindu divine ancestors — are, in JT's read, the original personalities seeded into the simulation, now scattered among us and mostly asleep to themselves (the guy behind the register at the 7-Eleven might be one). And the **tripartite nature of man** — the Gnostic hylic / psychic / pneumatic split — gets mapped onto the modern NPC discourse and onto Dean Radin's "psychic gene": a chromosome-7 mutation, carried by roughly 40% of the population and centered geographically on Rome, that correlates with a total absence of psychic perception and, JT suspects, clusters in positions of authority.

He closes personal: the 1993 Uranus–Neptune awakening, his childhood sense of an unmet mission, the dark-triad "social immune system" that isolates anyone spreading new awareness, and — candidly — the three sick cats he's trying to keep fed, which is the actual reason a "buy me a coffee" link will eventually show up. Not a grift; just a disabled guy who wants to keep talking about this and not be crushed by vet bills.

## Show notes

- **Peak Mind** = the period of peak aggregate attention (~late 2012–2019) directed through social media at the same archetypes daily; technology as a mirror/projection of human potential, strongest when we're on the *downward* arc of consciousness and least self-reliant
- **The revelation of the hierarchy** — a Theosophical prediction (peaked 1920s–30s) of an interstellar object arriving ~2025 as the **Kalki avatar**, transmitting a signal that triggers a civilizational reset; JT links it to the rider on the white horse in the Book of Revelation
- Theosophy framed as the last great clairvoyant community, born when Western Europeans reached Tibet in the 1860s; "1920s edgelords" who leaned into Luciferian ("light-bringer") language and got demonized by evangelical/conspiratainment culture
- **Simulation on biological hardware** — a networked set of living brains, not a silicon computer; the literal substrate of Jung's collective unconscious. Lineage traced through Nick Bostrom, Philip K. Dick's 1977 Paris talk, and far older Buddhist, Hindu, Greek, and Zoroastrian sources
- **Archons** = Gnostic rulers who hijacked humanity into the simulation and installed our vices; also mapped onto the planets and their "kingdoms," i.e. the ancient belief in inhabited worlds
- **Pitris** (Hindu divine ancestors) reinterpreted as the original individual consciousnesses seeded into the simulation — always "here" because here *is* them, but mostly unaware of their own identity; awakening = those personalities remembering themselves
- **Tripartite nature of man** — the Gnostic **hylic / psychic / pneumatic** hierarchy, offered as the ancestor of today's "NPC"; JT's extended defense that the term isn't inherently a slur, and that the 40% who "miss a sense" shouldn't be hated — "they are us and we are them"
- **The psychic gene** — Dean Radin / Institute of Noetic Sciences research on a chromosome-7 mutation (~40% prevalence) correlating with absent psychic ability, geographically centered on Rome; JT's inference that it's overrepresented up the authority ladder (police → teachers → doctors → academics → politicians)
- The **community of awakened beings** as a global distributed network (the Sangha around Buddha, the Ecclesia around Christ) — not a claim that one religion is right; JT identifies as Christian and explicitly not a cult leader
- The **dark triad** (Machiavellianism, narcissism, psychopathy) theorized as a social immune system that isolates and suppresses carriers of new ideas — i.e. anyone awakening
- Emerging archetypes: the ~2011 zombie-apocalypse craze read retroactively as a premonition of COVID lockdowns; the **simulation** as the current dominant emerging archetype now surfacing across TV, film, and games (e.g. *Fallout*)
- Origins tangent — the archaic revival via Aleister Crowley and the hermeticists (late-1800s Neptune–Pluto conjunction), 1950s channeling of the **Council of Nine**, and Gene Roddenberry drawing on that material for *Star Trek*; Diana Pasulka named as the credentialed academic voice
- Personal arc — the 1993 Uranus–Neptune conjunction and the New Age "utopian" 90s as JT's first direct perception of the simulation; a childhood of daily "I've lost another day" mission-anxiety and vivid space daydreams in the 1970s
- Format notes — recorded in Spaces for now "because I'm acting like a boomer"; targeting 75–90 minutes; the honest cat-rescue reason a modest monetization link is coming (three sick cats, disability, no grift intended)
