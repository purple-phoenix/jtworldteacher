## About this episode

The monthly **Astro RV** Space, with co-host Melissa. The core subject is **Ketu transiting Magha Nakshatra** from March 29 through the end of the year — a nine-month Vedic window for clearing ancestral karma. JT pairs it with his ongoing Saturn-conjunct-Neptune-at-zero-Aries thesis (the biggest Western transit in a century, signaling the collapse of global civilization, oil shortages after April 15 when the last Saudi shipments to JP Morgan land, war spread, and karmic deep impact).

The two systems converge on the same theme: illusions stripped away, corrupt leaders falling, false identities and unserving relationships dissolved. JT argues this is the late-Kali-Yuga phase where angels and demons inhabit the same bodies — Epstein-files-style exposure — followed by a separation into different worlds. He frames the work as personal: resentment and the desire for revenge are what tie the soul back into the reality overlay, so the next nine months are an unusually clean window to dissolve those bonds and move toward moksha.

There's a strong autobiographical thread. JT had a "Festivus" blowout with his late-seventies father hours before going live and uses it as the live case study — he observed his father as someone whose resentments control him rather than the reverse, an NPC running an ancestral script. He extends that to a general rule for the transit: stop relating to other people as individuals when they're actually being moved by transpersonal archetypes, and the karmic tie breaks.
