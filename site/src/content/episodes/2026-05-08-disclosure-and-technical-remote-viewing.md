## About this episode

JT's declarative thesis: the broader Disclosure movement is a controlled, deceptive rollout — and Technical Remote Viewing (TRV), particularly the Intuitive Underground (IU) canon work led by Morgan Farrell and Dan Mann, is the only methodology that actually answers what's going on. The "ultimate disclosure" isn't little gray men but a biotechnological reality overlay: we are real people stuck inside a collective dream/simulation generated on networked neural tissue, with the Cortical Labs human-brain-organoid computers in Melbourne and Singapore serving as real-world proof of concept. Jimmy Carter sobbed at his desk because he learned humanity was hijacked inside an alien experiment — and JT insists Burchette and the Greer-style "love and light" channelers are both running a controlled spin on that core truth.

Against this, JT positions UFOX, channelers, and the Stephen Greer faction as "conspiratainment" siloed into confirmation-bias camps — plasma beings, djinn, angels and demons, interdimensionals — none of which contradict TRV data but all of which get weaponized as sectarian distractions. He is openly hostile to the contemporary fad of demanding leaked classified U.S. military weapons tech, arguing any movement of 50,000+ people pushing for that release is necessarily salted with foreign intelligence assets; "we need the government to protect us" is the throughline. Morgan's IU side-project session on the latest released UAP video came back as "innocuous, man-made, military tech" inside a broader inoculation campaign.

The constructive program is escape: TRV plus historical/esoteric/religious data points to a coming reboot (the kill shot / magnetic pole shift, peak observation 2012-2019 now reversing into contraction), and the IU material covers moksha-style techniques — heart-mind coherence, reducing karmic entanglement, animal compassion — to avoid being recycled through the next iteration.
