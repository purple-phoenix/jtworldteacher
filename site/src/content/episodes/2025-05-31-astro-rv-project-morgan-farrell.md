## About this episode

The foundational Astro RV Project Space. JT sits with Technical Remote Viewer Morgan Farrell (Intuitive Underground) and Dan Mann to lay out the methodology: astrology as an analog simulation model built by the Babylonians over 500 years of "ordinary watching," used as a targeting layer to extend Technical Remote Viewing past its 6-to-18-month horizon. Astrology gives the timing; TRV (Ed Dames lineage) gives the event. Combined right, you get over-the-horizon predictive intelligence — the scientific descendant of what Nostradamus was doing when he targeted his clairvoyance with astrological transits.

The thesis is operational: with the right tasking, this rig can be aimed at things that actually matter — nuclear flashpoints, regime changes, agricultural collapse — and produce actionable data. JT and Morgan walk through their first joint experiment (Jupiter-Uranus, April 2024, anchored on the natal chart of the nuclear age) where the team hit a back-channel US-Russia de-escalation around the Iran-Israel weekend that, if Pepe Escobar's limited hangout is real, is "the most significant thing you've ever predicted." They also dissect a Dan Waits / Rockstar Intuition video — competent guys, terrible tasking — as a cautionary tale of what happens when influencers crank-handle a project before they've actually read the paper.
