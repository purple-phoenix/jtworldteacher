## About this episode

Underneath it all is the geopolitics: containment of China, with Venezuela, Cuba, Greenland, and Iran read as beachheads. JT closes on action items — get six months ahead on food, medicine, gas, and car repairs; gamify preparation; expect speech restrictions; maintain mobility.
