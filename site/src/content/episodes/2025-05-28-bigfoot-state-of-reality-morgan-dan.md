## About this episode

JT hosts Morgan Farrell and Dan Mann of Intuitive Underground — the Ed Dames-lineage Technical Remote Viewing team JT has been calling the Rosetta Stone for esoteric knowledge — and they walk through the Canon Project: ten-plus years of TRV sessions that converge, target after target, on the same picture. Humans were genetically engineered by non-human intelligences ("space dicks" / archons) as the spoils of a lost war, our base reality was hijacked and overwritten by a technological false-reality overlay, our soul energy powers the machine that imprisons us, and the catastrophic-reset / harvest cycle is ongoing — we are not three generations downstream from base reality, we are the ones inside the experiment right now. This is the long-form version of every thesis JT routes through on Against-the-Archons and the Astro-RV Project, told by the viewers whose data he treats as load-bearing.

Morgan and Dan open with the trigger warning: this material has put both of them through months of anxiety and depression, it shattered Morgan's worldview, and they will not soften it. JT frames the whole Space as Rosetta-Stone work — Intuitive Underground's modern, technological-vocabulary translation of what clairvoyants across 2,000+ years have been seeing in allegory and esoteric code, finally legible because these viewers know what a computer and a simulation are. The arc moves from the kidnap-and-engineer origin of homo sapiens sapiens, through the Black Iron Prison riot and Philip K. Dick correlation, into the false-reality overlay, the loosh / soul-energy harvest mechanism, the between-life trap zone that targets rebels and helpers specifically on re-entry, and lands on the only escape JT and Morgan agree on: develop your psychic faculty, work with your own consciousness, and avoid the light at death.

- The harvest cycle: pole shift / solar flare / cataclysm as the reset mechanism; populations farmed up to ~8 billion, "cage gets shook" to generate loosh, viewers who figure it out get weeded out

- Escape protocol: learn to be psychic, work with your consciousness, advanced consciousness tools, "avoid the light" at death; every being who backs out weakens the system for the next
