## About this episode

JT and Morgan Farrell (Intuitive Underground, co-originator of the Astro-RV Project) work through the thesis that most of what gets sold as "the paranormal" makes sense only once you accept two facts the public is never taught: we are inside a reality overlay, and human beings are a eusocial collective organism that has been domesticated. The UFO phenomenon, the Virgin Mary in abduction reports, Jacques Vallee's pancake-cooking elves, cryptids that pop in and out, the religious imagery that UFOX investigators routinely scrub from their write-ups — all of it is incoherent if you treat it as visitors from a planet, and tidy if you treat it as a mix of archonic intrusions from outside the overlay, archetypes welling up from a networked collective unconscious, and biological artifacts of the hybridization and domestication that produced modern humans 50,000 to 30,000 years ago.

From there the conversation widens into E. O. Wilson's suppressed eusociality work (why human brains shrank 11% the same way ant brains shrink when colonies form), the Soviet fox experiment as the Rosetta Stone for our own floppy ears, lost hair, and fused chromosome 2, Neanderthal/Denisovan introgression and Dragon Man, Julian Jaynes on the bicameral mind, the Cosmic Serpent's claim that DNA is the demiurge, and Nassim Haramein's feedback-mechanism argument for why distributed biological intelligence outruns any AI superintelligence by construction. JT closes by routing all of it back to his governing concern: alleviating suffering here and preparing for death — and naming the rising cognitive aggression inside UFOX (the Rogers coin payoffs, Red Panda Koala's "satanic hypnotists in a Winnebago" stories, the soul-domains panic) as the same overlay running its own reset.

- DNA as the demiurge (The Cosmic Serpent, Jeremy Narby) — biophotons, the snake in ayahuasca visions, and biocentrism as why the overlay is biological all the way down
