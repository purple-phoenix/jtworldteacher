## About this episode

JT opens the inaugural 2026 Space framing the year ahead through "Karmic Deep Impact" — his name for the Saturn-Neptune conjunction crystallizing at zero degrees Aries in late February, a once-in-6,400-year alignment landing nearly exact on the US natal IC. This isn't an abstract astrology lecture: it's the world hitting bottom. Karma here means consequences, not mysticism — the Saturnine crystallization of 36 years of unaddressed Neptunian delusion (woke ideology, internet self-disclosure, the OnlyFans economy, influencer culture, the fentanyl-zombie hollowing of American cities) into hard, inescapable material form.

The thesis: everything we've been intangibly accruing since the early 1990s — the data we typed into the void, the ideological bets we placed, the medical and economic decisions we deferred — is about to be scraped, profiled, and collected by AI as the literal mechanism of karmic return. The medical system tightening, the throttling on X, the Greenland rare-earth standoff, Russia's coming military ascendancy, the bifurcation of America when Uranus re-enters Gemini in summer — these are the same transit expressing on different scales. The window opens February 20, sharpens when Mars conjuncts the point in May, and the orb runs three years.

For most people this is a heavy burden period. For the spiritually receptive — and only a limited subset of people remain receptive, since "a lot of these fields aren't good for growing anything anymore" — there's a genuine possibility of a Saturn-Neptune spiritual awakening, downloads from the collective unconscious, possibly even Hopi-prophecy-grade cryptid materialization (a literal Bigfoot on the news would be the paranormal epitome of this transit). JT closes by pointing listeners toward Saturday's followup Space on "The Reality Hack" — strategic nonviolence and wu wei repackaged as a way to alter your circumstances without entreating the archons.
