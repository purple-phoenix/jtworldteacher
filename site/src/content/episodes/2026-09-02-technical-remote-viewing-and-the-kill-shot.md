## About this episode

Peak Mind comes back from a six-week Spaces outage with a guest: **Morgan Farrell** of **Intuitive Underground**, a remote viewing instructor who trained under **Ed Dames**. JT opens with some housekeeping — the elaborate plans for structuring the podcast had dug him into a hole, so the better move was to bring Morgan in "and start from that point and work forward" — and his brother Matt runs the interview from a set of prepared questions, asking the beginner's questions on purpose. Morgan starts with the history: parts of the government program, including the Stanford research through the '70s and '80s, were never classified; the operational unit at Fort Meade was declassified in 1995; Dames had founded his private company Psi Tech in 1989. Morgan himself found remote viewing in 2011, laid up after a construction injury, bought Dames' home-study course to test it for himself, "and never looked back."

The middle of the episode is a plain-language primer. "Remote viewing" names both a **protocol** (a tasker, a viewer kept blind, an arbitrary eight-digit target reference number, feedback afterward) and a **method**. Morgan traces the method from free-form natural psychics, through Hal Puthoff and **Ingo Swann** reverse-engineering Swann's process into the staged **CRV** (coordinate, later controlled, remote viewing), to Dames' more rigid and streamlined **technical remote viewing**: rapid-fire prompt and response, alternating sketching and describing through higher stages as the "psychic aperture" opens, ending in a site template and written summary. JT adds that TRV's care with how a target is worded — what Morgan calls "the syntax and grammar of the unconscious mind" — looks a lot like how a context engineer gets good results out of an AI, and speculates (flagging it as speculation) that Dames understood that. Matt's *Stranger Things* question gets a real answer: **bilocation**, remote viewing as "an attention management skill," why extended remote viewing is more experiential and TRV deliberately holds the target at arm's length — and JT's aside on the Montauk rumors said to have inspired the show.

The last third takes on the **Kill Shot** — the large-scale disruption Dames' viewers started picking up in the late '80s and refined to an Earth-facing X-class solar flare, with the 1859 **Carrington Event** as the precedent and a grid full of two-year-lead-time transformers as the vulnerability. JT recalls Dames as "Dr. Doom" on *Coast to Coast AM*, concedes the timing mistake everyone made ("the end of the world is taking forever"), and argues the prediction hasn't failed — its precondition, the early stage of a global conflict, has never existed until now. Dames died earlier this year, and JT pays tribute. The close goes further out: a "call and response" between the sun and the Earth's inhabitants, a solar event as the thing that stops something worse, and JT's idea that life exists to anticipate environmental change — which would make the Kill Shot material "an extremely important transmission from the collective unconscious."

## Show notes

- **Housekeeping** — back after X took JT's Spaces away for about six weeks; dropping the elaborate episode plan, randomizing topics instead, and starting the story from Morgan and working forward
- **How remote viewing went public** — unclassified research at Stanford through the '70s and '80s; the classified operations unit at Fort Meade, Maryland; full declassification in August 1995; Ed Dames founding Psi Tech in 1989 as a contractor before opening it to the public
- **Morgan's way in** — a workplace back injury in union construction, time off in 2011, rekindled interest in "the nature of our reality," and the realization that research is only as good as its author; buying Dames' home-study course to see for himself
- **Protocol vs. method** — tasker/project manager and viewer; the arbitrary eight-digit **target reference number**; keeping the viewer blind; verifiable training targets (the Eiffel Tower example); contemporaneous transcript of descriptors and sketches; unblinding and feedback
- **From natural psychics to a teachable system** — control subjects performing as well as the psychics once shown how; Hal Puthoff and **Ingo Swann** reverse-engineering Swann's process into staged **coordinate/controlled remote viewing (CRV)**; signal-to-noise control
- **Technical remote viewing (TRV)** — Dames' private-sector refinement of CRV: more rigidly structured but streamlined; rapid-fire prompt and response at a "metronomic pace"; stage 1 contact sketch, stage 2 low-level sensory and dimensional data, the "psychic aperture" widening by stage 4, then the site template and written summary
- **"The syntax and grammar of the unconscious mind"** — why TRV target wording is tight and strategic where other schools' taskings read, in JT's phrase, like "something between a story and a spell"
- **JT's AI analogy** — TRV tasking compared to context engineering (limited context window, avoiding hallucination); JT's flagged speculation that Dames understood how AI works; Morgan: no firsthand knowledge, but it "does track logically"
- Why TRV fell out of public favor, and JT's bet that people fluent in the emerging technology will gravitate back to it
- **Matt's *Stranger Things* question** — what a session is actually like; **bilocation** and remote viewing as an attention-management skill; extended remote viewing (ERV) as the more experiential, dreamlike, monochrome mode; TRV's roughly 50/50 split of attention; clairsentience and claircognizance; why the more experiential a session gets, the more subjective it becomes
- The **Montauk** rumors and their alleged link to *Stranger Things* — flagged by JT as rumor; Morgan suggests a colleague, Theodore, as a future guest on the subject
- **The Kill Shot** — the late-'80s data on a large-scale disruption refined to an Earth-directed X-class flare / coronal mass ejection; the **1859 Carrington Event** overloading telegraph systems; an unhardened modern grid and a two-year manufacturing wait for main transformers
- **"Dr. Doom"** on *Coast to Coast AM*; the imminence mistake; how selling preparedness and "best place to be" sessions got the TRV community called a grift
- **Precursor events** JT recalls from Dames' material — the mRNA vaccination sessions (event, not endorsement), a nuclear weapon on the Korean peninsula, a shuttle forced home by solar activity, a large culling of cattle herds, and above all the onset of **World War III–like conditions**; Morgan: "it's all basically correct"
- **Ed Dames' death** earlier this year and JT's tribute; JT's argument that the preconditions for the Kill Shot have never existed before now
- Morgan teases upcoming work after a long conversation with Dames' longest-serving Matrix Intelligence Agency team member
- **Sun and consciousness** — the '90s-era, Mesoamerican-flavored idea of a feedback loop between solar events and human consciousness; TRV work describing a "call and response" between the Earth's inhabitants and the sun; JT's sci-fi framing of a solar event stopping "tech bros" from turning humanity into the Borg
- **JT's closing frame** — life as the anticipation of environmental change; history read as "current events radiating back into the past"; remote viewing's public emergence as preparation, which makes the Kill Shot material "an extremely important transmission from the collective unconscious"
- Next time with Morgan: the Intuitive Underground material
